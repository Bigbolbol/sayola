# Sayola

Aid and remittances reach Syria in seconds. Sayola makes sure the cash is there too, funded by halal income from families' savings.

Sayola extends the [Stellar Disbursement Platform](https://stellar.org/products-and-tools/disbursement-platform) with:

- **Booked cash-out with proof of delivery**: funds are held for the recipient in a Soroban contract and released to the agent once both sides confirm the cash handed over.
- **A halal liquidity vault**: savers fund agents' cash float under a musharakah (profit-and-loss sharing) contract and earn a share of real cash-out fees, with no guaranteed return.
- **Payday forecasting**: scheduled disbursements are used to position cash with agents before demand peaks.

Recipients can collect with a smartphone, a basic phone (SMS one-time code), or a printed card and PIN, or through a registered proxy. No personal data is stored on-chain.

## Status
